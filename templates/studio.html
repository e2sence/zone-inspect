6 · Calibrate

Calibration learns what normal variation looks like from your good samples and checks every defect sample against it. Each good sample is compared with all the others, so the report shows how many false alarms to expect on good boards. A defect is caught when its score is above the OK limit. Takes from several seconds to a minute.
